# Project Name & Positioning

## Name
**RecrUnion**

## Positioning statement
**RecrUnion is an AI-powered recruitment and interview intelligence platform that helps hiring teams screen candidates, conduct evidence-guided interviews, and produce transparent, evidence-backed assessments.**

## Name rationale
The name combines recruitment with the idea of bringing fragmented hiring evidence together into one coherent process. RecrUnion connects job requirements, CV evidence, interview transcripts, scoring, and final assessment instead of treating them as separate activities. The product identity emphasizes coordination and evidence-based decision support rather than fully automated hiring.

## Product principles
- human decision remains final
- evidence over opaque AI judgment
- consistent evaluation against job requirements
- traceable CV/interview conclusions
- privacy-aware handling of candidate data
